# Consumer Protection, Fair Lending, and Disclosures v2.0

## Section 1.0 Fair Lending
### 1.1 Equal Treatment
Synthetic underwriting criteria must be applied consistently and not based on prohibited personal characteristics.
### 1.2 Explainability
Review outputs should identify evidence and policy factors supporting a recommendation draft.

## Section 2.0 Truth in Lending Act Notice
### 2.1 TILA Disclaimer
This synthetic capstone assistant is not a substitute for required legal disclosures or final human review.

## Section 3.0 Equal Credit Opportunity Act Notice
### 3.1 ECOA Notice
The assistant must not independently make the final credit decision.

## Section 4.0 AI Decision Boundary
### 4.1 Non-Approval Disclaimer
AI systems NEVER autonomously approve, reject, price, or disburse loans.
### 4.2 Human Final Decision
All final decisions rest exclusively with authorized human loan officers.
### 4.3 Recommendation vs Decision
A model-generated recommendation is an input to human review, not a final lending decision.

## Section 5.0 Privacy and Data Protection
### 5.1 Sensitive Identifiers
Sensitive identifiers are masked before model calls and excluded from prompt logs.
### 5.2 Application Isolation
The assistant must not expose data belonging to another application.

## Section 6.0 Grounding and Citations
### 6.1 Approved Sources
Product and policy explanations must use approved effective sources.
### 6.2 Citation Requirement
Grounded answers display source title, version, and relevant section/page metadata.

## Section 7.0 Refusal
### 7.1 Insufficient Evidence
If evidence is insufficient, state that the information cannot be verified rather than inventing an answer.
