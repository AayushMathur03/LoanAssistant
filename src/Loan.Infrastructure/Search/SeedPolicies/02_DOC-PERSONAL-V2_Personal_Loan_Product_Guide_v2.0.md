# Personal Loan Product Guide v2.0

## Section 1.0 Product Overview
### 1.1 Product Purpose
The Synthetic Bank Personal Loan is an unsecured installment-loan product for eligible synthetic applicants. Fictional capstone content only.
### 1.2 Maximum Principal
Maximum principal: $75,000.
### 1.3 Security
The product remains unsecured.

## Section 2.0 Eligibility Requirements
### 2.1 Debt-to-Income Ratio
Maximum standard DTI: 38.0%.
### 2.2 Credit Score
A minimum credit score of 600 may be considered for preferred tiers, subject to applicable controls.
### 2.3 Monthly Income
Minimum gross monthly income: $2,500.

## Section 3.0 Loan Terms
### 3.1 Available Terms
Terms range from 12 through 72 months.

## Section 4.0 Changes from v1.0
### 4.1 Maximum Principal Change
Maximum principal increased from $50,000 to $75,000.
### 4.2 DTI Change
Standard maximum DTI increased from 36.0% to 38.0%.
### 4.3 Credit Score Change
The minimum threshold changed from 620 to 600 for preferred tiers, subject to controls.
### 4.4 Income Change
Minimum monthly income increased from $2,000 to $2,500.
### 4.5 Term Change
Maximum term increased from 60 to 72 months.

## Section 5.0 Review and Decision Boundaries
### 5.1 Human Review
The assistant prepares information for review and cannot approve or reject a loan.
### 5.2 Citation
Grounded answers must cite the effective policy version and relevant section/page metadata.
