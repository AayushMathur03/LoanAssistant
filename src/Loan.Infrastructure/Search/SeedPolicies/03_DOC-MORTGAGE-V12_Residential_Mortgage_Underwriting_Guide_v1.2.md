# Residential Mortgage Underwriting Guide v1.2

## Section 1.0 Product Overview
### 1.1 Product Scope
The Synthetic Bank Standard Residential Mortgage is a secured synthetic home-loan scenario for the capstone.
### 1.2 Maximum Principal
Maximum standard principal: $750,000.

## Section 2.0 Affordability Controls
### 2.1 Maximum DTI
Standard maximum DTI: 43.0%.
### 2.2 DTI Referral
DTI above 43.0% requires human loan-officer referral and manual review.
### 2.3 Deterministic Calculation
DTI is calculated by deterministic application-domain logic from confirmed facts and approved verification results.

## Section 3.0 Loan-to-Value Controls
### 3.1 Maximum LTV Without PMI
Maximum LTV without PMI: 80.0%.
### 3.2 PMI Waiver
A minimum 20% down payment is required to waive PMI under this synthetic policy.

## Section 4.0 Credit Requirements
### 4.1 Minimum Credit Score
Minimum credit score: 640.

## Section 5.0 Manual Review
### 5.1 Referral Conditions
Missing evidence, conflicting evidence, DTI above threshold, or exceptions require human review.
### 5.2 Decision Boundary
The assistant prepares a recommendation but does not approve or reject the mortgage.

### 6.1 Source Citation
Policy-based responses must identify the effective version and relevant section/page metadata.
