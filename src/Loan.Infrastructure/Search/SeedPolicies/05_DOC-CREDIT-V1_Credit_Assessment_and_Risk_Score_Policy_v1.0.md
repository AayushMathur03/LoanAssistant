# Credit Assessment and Risk Score Policy v1.0

## Section 1.0 Purpose
### 1.1 Objective
This synthetic policy defines credit tiers and selected risk controls.

## Section 2.0 Credit Score Tiers
### 2.1 Tier 1 — Excellent
740+ = Tier 1 — Excellent.
### 2.2 Tier 2 — Good
680–739 = Tier 2 — Good.
### 2.3 Tier 3 — Fair
640–679 = Tier 3 — Fair.
### 2.4 Tier 4 — High Risk / Referral
600–639 = Tier 4 — High Risk / Referral.
### 2.5 Below 600
Below 600 = Ineligible under this synthetic policy.

## Section 3.0 Delinquency Controls
### 3.1 Open Delinquencies
Maximum allowable open delinquencies: 0 in the past 24 months.

## Section 4.0 Bankruptcy Control
### 4.1 Waiting Period
Minimum bankruptcy waiting period: 4 years.

## Section 5.0 Verification
### 5.1 Source
Credit information must come from approved timestamped synthetic verification tools or confirmed fields.
### 5.2 Tool Failure
If credit is unavailable, mark the factor unavailable. Never invent a score.

## Section 6.0 Decision Boundary
### 6.1 Human Review
The assistant may explain credit tiers but cannot independently approve or reject a loan.
