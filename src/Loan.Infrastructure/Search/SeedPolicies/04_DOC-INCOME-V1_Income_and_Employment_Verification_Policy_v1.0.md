# Income and Employment Verification Policy v1.0

## Section 1.0 Purpose
### 1.1 Objective
This synthetic policy defines minimum income and employment evidence.

## Section 2.0 Salaried Applicant Evidence
### 2.1 Recent Paystubs
Two most recent consecutive paystubs are mandatory.
### 2.2 Recency
Each paystub must be dated within the last 30 days.

## Section 3.0 Historical Income Evidence
### 3.1 W-2 Requirement
W-2 forms for the past two years are required when applicable.
### 3.2 Self-Employed Applicants
Self-employed applicants require two years of tax returns when applicable.

## Section 4.0 Employment Verification
### 4.1 Verification
Employer phone verification or digital payroll-provider confirmation is required.
### 4.2 Timestamp
Verification results must be timestamped and linked to the application.

## Section 5.0 Missing or Conflicting Evidence
### 5.1 Missing Evidence
Missing mandatory evidence remains unresolved and must not be invented.
### 5.2 Conflicts
Conflicting evidence requires additional review; the assistant must not silently choose a preferred value.

## Section 6.0 Provenance
### 6.1 Confirmed Facts
Income used in eligibility must come from confirmed fields or approved timestamped verification results.
### 6.2 Field Provenance
Extracted income values retain their source document and location metadata.
